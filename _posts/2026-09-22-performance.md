Growing up, I've always performed. I was a good student every year. I got scholarships. My parents could brag. But part of the performance was hiding things. Hiding pain. Hiding feelings and emotional needs. Hiding when I needed to be seen.

But that's not what matters in life, you dummy! Your self-worth is not tied to your extrinsic successes. It's intrinsic. We love you for you, not for the stats. No matter what. If you're broken, you're broken.

You have always been who you are due to the way you connect with others. Your relationships. The love you give. The way you make people feel and the way people make you feel. That's what makes your life so impactful. Not the numbers in your bank account. Human connection is the most powerful thing we have, for it is the only thing we can all have.

To be "cringe" is to be free. What's the downside to vulnerability? People might judge you for it. The upside is so much more powerful.
