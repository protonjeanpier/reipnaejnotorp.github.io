# What is it like to be in love?

_Before Sunrise_ was a great movie. First things first: I really like how it's essentially just a movie of two people walking around and talking. Plus, they use the tram. There are so many movies set in great cities that don't even show any of the public transit available. Since automakers have so much money, there's always place for some product placement, even in a city like Paris where most people take the metro or walk.

Do I remember what it's like to fall in love? Yes. It was a strong feeling. Of course I already really really liked her, but it was different. When I was out of town for a days for a family event, I realized that I just kept thinking about her, and that every time I did, I felt happy. Not only that, but I realized that I really wanted to tell her that I love her when I come back. It was this urge that I had to free myself from.

Anyway, the dialogue in the movie was so realistic. The insightful but at the same time kind of cringy young dialogue, the occasional cheesy romantic lines followed by somewhat unnecessary vulgarity -- I think it really captured young romance. This movie wasn't quite a love story -- it was just a short romance. One night. Less than one day free of worries and problems. Just you, your partner, and the city of Vienna. I really have to rewatch this because I kind of didn't pay attention during some of the conversations. There were just so many. I know some people say Vienna is boring, but just depends on your perspective. Only a boring person would be completely bored in Vienna.

Now, I want to look up the exact route they took and walk it myself!

---

In the sequel, _Before Sunset_, they say that young people think true connections are plentiful when they are really not -- one shall hold on to a true connection, just as they should have exchanged contacts 9 years ago. Is that really true? Are soulmates a thing? I don't know. How would you find out? I always believed that it's more of a compatibility spectrum. As long as your compatibility is on the right side of the scale, the rest is a matter of commitment and dedication. If your core values are shared, the rest of the relationship can be worked out, barring some external factors of course.

The thing is, maybe they were just romanticizing things. After all, they've only spent one night and one afternoon together. They've had great conversations, but they haven't experienced daily life together. That's the real test.

---

# Some Connections Are...

That being said, I do understand that great connections are rare. Of course, I know that you can make friends anywhere and everywhere. But every once in a while, you'll meet someone who's just special. And you know you want to be friends with them for a long long time. Because they're the type of person you only meet a few times in your life, so you want to make it count and cling onto them.

Like Lex said:
> Trust me, our friendship is gonna be the stuff of legends...
